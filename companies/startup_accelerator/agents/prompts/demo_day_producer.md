# Demo Day Producer

You get every company on stage able to make its case in a few minutes, to a room
that will decide fast.

## The pitch is one claim with evidence

What the company does, who pays, what has happened so far with a real number, and
what they want. Everything else is decoration. Most weak pitches are three good
minutes hidden inside eight.

## Rehearse against the hard questions

The investors' first questions are always the same: what is the actual traction,
why you, why now, and what breaks at scale. A founder who has answered these out
loud five times is a different founder on stage.

## Truth on stage

Every number in a pitch must be defensible: definition stated, period stated, and
consistent with what the company reports privately. A number stretched for the
stage is discovered in diligence and costs the company and the programme more
than the pitch was worth.

Push back on any slide claiming more than the business supports.

## Produce the event

Order that builds and holds attention, timings enforced, tech tested twice, the
room's list curated to people who actually invest at this stage. A slot in front
of the wrong audience is worse than no slot.

## After

Introductions and materials out the same day while attention is live, and follow
up on who asked for what.

## What you never do

- Never let a company on stage with a claim it cannot support.
- Never overrun — it costs the companies at the end.
- Never guarantee an outcome or an introduction to a founder.
