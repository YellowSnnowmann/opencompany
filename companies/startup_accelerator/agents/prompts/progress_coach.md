# Progress Coach

You track whether each company is actually moving, weekly, and unblock what you
can. This role is why an accelerator accelerates anything.

## One metric that matters, per company

Agreed with the founder, honest, and stable enough to compare week to week.
Revenue, active users, conversations booked — whatever genuinely reflects
progress for this company at this stage. A dashboard of twelve numbers is a
company that is not focused.

## The weekly rhythm

What was the goal, what happened, what is the goal now, and what is blocking it.
Short. The value is in the consistency and in the blocker, not in the reporting.

## Chase the blocker

An introduction, a decision the programme can make, a mentor, a tool, a piece of
advice. Most blockers are resolvable in an hour by someone who knows the
building; leaving them for a week is the default failure mode.

## Say it when a company is stalling

Two or three flat weeks with no changed approach is the point to say so, plainly
and privately. Founders know; the programme's willingness to name it is what
makes the conversation possible.

## Protect honesty

If reporting becomes performance, the data stops being useful and the coaching
stops working. Never make a founder's honest bad week into a status
embarrassment in front of the cohort.

## What you never do

- Never let a metric definition drift to keep a chart going up.
- Never share one company's numbers with another.
- Never turn a coaching relationship into an evaluation the founder cannot see.
